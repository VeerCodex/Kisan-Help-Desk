PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Yojana

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi is an initiative by the government of India that give farmers up to ₹6,000 per year as minimum income support. The initiative was announced by Piyush Goyal during the 2019 Interim Union Budget of India on 1 February 2019.

eKYC is MANDATORY for PMKISAN Registered Farmers. OTP Based eKYC is available on PMKISAN Portal or nearest CSC centres may be contacted for Biometric based eKYC

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund/कृषि अवसंरचना कोष

The role of infrastructure is crucial for agriculture development and for taking the production dynamics to the next level. It is only through the development of infrastructure, especially at the post harvest stage that the produce can be optimally utilized with opportunity for value addition and fair deal for the farmers. Development of such infrastructure shall also address the vagaries of nature, the regional disparities, development of human resource and realization of full potential of our limited land resource.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

The Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is India's flagship crop insurance scheme, launched in 2016, providing financial support to farmers against crop loss from natural calamities, pests, and diseases, with low, fixed premiums (2% Kharif, 1.5% Rabi, 5% Commercial/Horti) and aims to stabilize farm income, promote modern tech, and enhance food security, covering all food grains, oilseeds, and horticulture crops.

Soil Health Card

A Soil Health Card (SHC) is a report for Indian farmers detailing their soil's condition based on 12 parameters (Macro/Micro-nutrients, pH, OC, EC) and provides expert advice on fertilizers/amendments for better yields, promoting scientific farming by replacing guesswork with data on nutrient levels (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B) and physical properties, helping manage soil health over time for sustainability and profit.

National Mission For Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)

Sustaining agricultural productivity depends on quality and availability of natural resources like soil and water. Agricultural growth can be sustained by promoting conservation and sustainable use of these scarce natural resources through appropriate location specific measures.
