About Kisan Help Desk

Kisan Help Desk is a practical digital platform built to make modern agricultural guidance simple and local. We convert science-based advice into short, actionable steps farmers can use right now.

We cover crop planning, input guidance, weather alerts and local marketplace signals to help farmers take better decisions and reduce risk.

Our Mission

To empower farmers with timely, localized, and easy-to-follow recommendations that increase productivity and income while reducing losses and environmental impact.

What we provide

Crop Advisory

Seasonal, location-specific crop recommendations: variety, sowing, spacing and irrigation tips.

Fertilizer & Pesticide Guidance

Correct dosage, timing and safety steps to protect crops and farmers.

Weather Alerts

Short-term forecasts and alerts with clear action points for field operations.

Marketplace

Price signals and demand info to help you sell at the right time and place.

Our Vision

A future where every farmer has access to clear, science-backed advice — leading to higher yields, lower losses and more sustainable livelihoods.
