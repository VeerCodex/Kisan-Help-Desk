Crop Advisory — Practical guidance for farmers

We give short, localised, season-wise advice so you can choose right variety, sow at the right time and manage inputs efficiently.

Below are focused advisory modules — each module slides in from left or right as you scroll. Click "Open Form" to submit your field details for personalised recommendations.

Sowing & Variety Selection

Choose varieties adapted to your zone. Sow at the recommended window to avoid heat or unseasonal rains. Maintain recommended spacing for yield and disease control.

  • Check certified seed sources
  • Sow within the recommended sowing window for your district
  • Follow spacing guidelines to reduce pest pressure

Irrigation & Water Management

Use soil moisture checks and simple rules of thumb to schedule irrigation — avoid overwatering, maintain aeration and reduce fertilizer losses.

  • Check soil by touch (2–4 inch depth)
  • Prefer split irrigations over heavy single doses
  • Mulch to conserve moisture

Pest & Disease Management

Monitor fields weekly. Use threshold-based action: only spray when pests exceed economic thresholds and prefer biocontrols where possible.

  • Field scouting once a week
  • Use pheromone traps for monitoring
  • Follow recommended protective equipment and safe dosages

Fertilizer Guidance

Right dose at the right time. Use soil test results where available; otherwise follow crop-specific blanket recommendations adapted to growth stage.

  • Top-dress nitrogen as per crop stage
  • Split doses reduce losses
  • Apply micronutrients if deficiency symptoms appear
