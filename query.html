Common FAQs & Solutions

Q: How to protect standing crops from sudden frost in winter?
Apply light irrigation in the evening. Silt/water retains heat, protecting the roots. Also, create mild smoke clouds around the field boundary if frost warning is high.
Q: What is the optimal treatment for Termite attack in wheat fields?
Mix Chlorpyriphos 20 EC with sand/irrigation water. Avoid using undecomposed cow dung ( गोबर खाद ) as it attracts termites.
Q: When should the first irrigation (CRI stage) be given to Wheat?
Crown Root Initiation (CRI) occurs at 21-25 days after sowing. This is the most critical irrigation stage for yield.
